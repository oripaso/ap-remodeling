What general contracting actually buys you

On a remodel, the expensive failures are almost never craftsmanship. They are sequencing — tile arriving before the waterproofing is signed off, an electrician booked for a day the drywall is not ready, a cabinet order placed against a measurement that changed. Someone has to hold that whole sequence. If you hire trades individually, that someone is you.

  • Full home remodeling — multi-room and whole-house work run as a single programme.
  • Kitchen remodels — layout, services, cabinetry, surfaces and finish.
  • Bathroom remodels — waterproofing corrected before anything decorative goes back.
  • Additions and reconfigurations — where the footprint or the internal walls change.
  • General contracting — scheduling, trades, materials, inspections and cleanup.

Scope before demolition

A&P writes the scope before work starts: what is included, what is explicitly not, and what would move the number. Change orders happen on real projects — a wall opens and something behind it is not what anyone expected — but they should be the exception you were warned about, not the business model.

Living in the house while it happens

Most remodels in Los Angeles happen with the family still in the building. That changes how the job is staged: dust control, which bathroom stays live, where materials sit, what time the crew starts. It is worth saying out loud at the estimate stage, and we will ask.

Inside and outside at once

If the roof is near the end of its life or the exterior needs work anyway, doing it alongside the remodel avoids paying twice for access, protection and mobilisation. Because A&P covers the exterior envelope too, that can be one estimate instead of two.
