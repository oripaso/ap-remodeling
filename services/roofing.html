What A&P does on a roof

Most roof problems in Los Angeles are not the field of the roof — they are the edges. Flashing at a chimney, a valley that was never lapped correctly, a low-slope section that ponds, a vent boot that dried out in ten summers of Valley heat. Those are the details that decide whether a roof leaks, and they are the details A&P looks at first.

  • Roof repair and maintenance — leaks, lifted or missing shingles, flashing, vent boots, storm and wind damage.
  • Roof replacement and tear-off — the old system stripped to the deck so the sheathing underneath can actually be inspected.
  • New roof installation — underlayment, flashing and finish material installed to manufacturer specification.
  • Leak diagnosis — finding where the water enters, which is rarely where it appears inside.

Materials we work with

Asphalt and composition shingle, concrete and clay tile, flat and low-slope systems, and metal roofing. Los Angeles housing stock mixes all of them, sometimes on the same house, and each fails in a different way. If you do not know what is on your roof, that is normal — we identify it on the visit and explain what your realistic options are, including the option to repair rather than replace.

Repair or replace?

A good contractor should be willing to tell you the repair is enough. Age of the system, condition of the deck, how many layers are already up there and how many separate leaks you have had all feed into that answer. You get it in writing, with pricing for each path where both are genuinely viable.
