What this site collects

The estimate form on this site does not send data to a server. When you complete it, your browser opens a pre-filled text message or email addressed to A&P, which you choose whether to send. Nothing is transmitted unless you send it. Any photos you attach stay on your device until you attach them to that message.

When you call, text, email or send the pre-filled message, we receive whatever you chose to include — typically your name, phone number, the property location and a description of the work. We use it only to respond to your enquiry, prepare an estimate and carry out work you engage us for.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. We share it only with subcontractors, suppliers or inspectors where that is necessary to quote or deliver your project, and where the law requires it.
