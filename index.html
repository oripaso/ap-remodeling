Why A&P

Most of what goes wrong on a job isn't the construction.

It's scope nobody wrote down, trades nobody coordinated, and a homeowner nobody called back. We run the parts that usually fail.

01

Clear scope before work starts

You know what is being done, what it costs and roughly how long it takes — in writing, before anyone lifts a tool.

02

One team, one point of contact

No homeowner should be project-managing five trades. That coordination is our job, and it is included.

03

Respect for your property

The house is protected while the work happens, and the site is swept and cleared before the crew leaves.

04

Communication all the way through

You should always know what happens next. You hear from us before you have to chase us.

05

Built for Los Angeles houses

Valley heat, low-slope roofs, stucco and wood siding, winter rain that arrives all at once — the local failure points are the ones we plan for.

06

Details, not decoration

Flashing, prep, waterproofing, sealing. The work nobody photographs is the work that decides how long the rest of it lasts.

How it goes

Four steps. No surprises.

  1. 01

    Tell us about the project

    A phone call or the form below. What is happening, where the house is, and roughly when you would like to start. No pressure at this stage.

  2. 02

    On-site consultation

    We come out and look properly — on the roof, behind the siding, under the deck — instead of quoting from the driveway. That is where the real scope shows up.

  3. 03

    Scope, estimate and timeline

    A written scope with pricing and an estimated schedule. What is included, what is not, and what would change the number. You decide on your own time.

  4. 04

    Construction and completion

    The crew arrives on schedule, protects the property, and builds it. You get updates as it moves. The site is cleaned before we call it finished.
